FAQ

How do leads work?

When a request matches your categories, region and accreditation, we send you the requester's details so you can quote directly. Featured and Premium labs receive priority routing.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. Paid plans are month-to-month and can be cancelled anytime.

Do you verify accreditation?

Yes. We check certificates and scopes with the issuing accreditation body before awarding verification badges.
