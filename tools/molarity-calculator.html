How to use the molarity calculator

Choose Find mass to calculate how many grams of solute you need for a target concentration and volume, or Find molarity if you already weighed your compound. Use the formula-lookup button to compute molecular weight automatically.

Molarity formula

mass (g) = concentration (mol/L) × volume (L) × molecular weight (g/mol)

Worked example

To prepare 500 mL of 0.1 M NaCl (MW 58.44 g/mol): 0.1 × 0.5 × 58.44 = 2.922 g. Dissolve in ~400 mL water, then bring to exactly 500 mL in a volumetric flask.

Pro tip: For hydrated salts (e.g. CuSO₄·5H₂O) use the MW of the hydrate you actually weigh, not the anhydrous form.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between molarity and molality?

Molarity is moles of solute per litre of solution (mol/L); molality is moles per kilogram of solvent (mol/kg). Molality does not change with temperature.

Why bring to volume instead of adding a fixed volume of water?

The solute occupies volume. Dissolving then filling to the mark in a volumetric flask gives an exact final volume and therefore an accurate concentration.

How do I convert mM to M?

Divide by 1,000. 250 mM = 0.25 M.
