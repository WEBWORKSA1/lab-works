How it works

Enter the number of reactions, reaction volume and overage. The calculator uses common final concentrations — 1× buffer, 0.2 mM each dNTP, 0.5 µM each primer, 1.25 U polymerase per 50 µL — and fills the rest with water. Always follow your enzyme supplier's recommendations.

Add 5–10% overage to cover pipetting loss. Keep the mix on ice and add the polymerase last.

Frequently asked questions

Why prepare a master mix?

It reduces pipetting steps, improves consistency between reactions and saves reagents.

Should template go in the master mix?

No — add template separately to each tube so you can include different samples and a no-template control.
