Formula

cells/mL = (cells counted ÷ squares counted) × dilution factor × 10⁴

Each large square of a Neubauer hemocytometer holds 0.1 µL (10⁻⁴ mL). With trypan blue mixed 1:1, the dilution factor is 2.

Viability

viability % = live ÷ (live + dead) × 100

Frequently asked questions

How many cells should I count?

Aim for 100–300 cells total across the squares for statistical reliability; dilute or concentrate if needed.

Which cells on the border do I count?

Use a consistent rule — typically count cells touching the top and left lines but not the bottom and right.
