The dilution equation

C₁ × V₁ = C₂ × V₂

C₁ is stock concentration, V₁ the volume of stock you take, C₂ the final concentration and V₂ the final total volume. Leave the unknown blank and the calculator solves it.

Worked example

You need 50 mL of 1× buffer from a 10× stock: V₁ = (1 × 50) / 10 = 5 mL of stock + 45 mL water.

Units of C₁ and C₂ must match, and units of V₁ and V₂ must match.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use C1V1 = C2V2 for percentages?

Yes, as long as both concentrations use the same unit (% w/v, mg/mL, M, ×, etc.).

What is a dilution factor?

The ratio of final volume to aliquot volume (V₂/V₁) — equivalently C₁/C₂. A 1:10 dilution has a dilution factor of 10.
