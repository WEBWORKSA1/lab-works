FAQ

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Lab.Works is an independent project, not a registered charity, so donations are generally not tax-deductible. Corporate sponsorships may qualify as marketing expenses — ask your accountant.

Can I give monthly?

Yes. Choose monthly in your payment method, or pledge below and we'll send a secure recurring link.

How will my contribution be used?

For operations and hosting, new calculators and guides, video production, promotion and marketing, hiring contributors, and funding contest prizes.

Can I get a refund?

Contact us within 14 days of a one-time donation and we'll refund it, no questions asked.
