A260/A280 & A260/A230 Ratios Explained: Is Your DNA Pure?

What spectrophotometer purity ratios mean, what causes bad ratios, and how to fix them.

By the Lab.Works Editorial Team · Updated September 2026 · Editorial policy

UV spectrophotometry is the quickest way to estimate nucleic-acid concentration and purity.

Concentration

ng/µL = A260 × 50 (dsDNA) | × 40 (RNA) | × 33 (ssDNA)

Purity ratios

RatioTargetLow value suggests
A260/A280~1.8 DNA, ~2.0 RNAProtein, phenol
A260/A2302.0–2.2Guanidine, EDTA, carbohydrates, phenol

Fixes

  • Extra wash steps on silica columns to remove guanidine
  • Ethanol precipitation to remove salts
  • RNase treatment if the 260/280 of DNA is > 2.0
  • Blank with the same buffer your sample is in
