RPM vs RCF: Why Your Protocol Says ×g (and How to Convert)

Why g-force matters more than RPM, how to find rotor radius, and a quick conversion reference.

By the Lab.Works Editorial Team · Updated September 2026 · Editorial policy

Two centrifuges spinning at the same RPM can apply very different forces. That's why good protocols specify relative centrifugal force (RCF, × g).

RCF = 1.118 × 10⁻⁵ × r × RPM²

Safety

  • Always balance tubes by mass, not just volume.
  • Never exceed the rotor's rated speed; derate for dense solutions.
  • Use sealed buckets for biohazards and wait for the rotor to stop completely.
