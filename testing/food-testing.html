Frequently asked questions

Can I calculate a nutrition label instead of lab testing?

Many regulators accept database calculations for nutrition labels, but lab analysis is more accurate for novel recipes, processed products and claims like 'high protein' or 'low sugar'.

What's the difference between a pathogen screen and an indicator test?

Pathogen tests look for specific organisms such as Salmonella or Listeria. Indicator tests (total plate count, coliforms, yeast & mould) measure overall hygiene and spoilage risk.

How long does a shelf-life study take?

Real-time studies take as long as the intended shelf life. Accelerated studies at elevated temperature can shorten this but must be validated for your product.
