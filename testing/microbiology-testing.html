Who uses it: Food and pharma QA teams, hospitals, cleanroom operators, disinfectant and textile brands.

Standards & accreditation

ISO 4833, USP 61/62/71, ISO 22196, EN 1276/13727, AOAC 955.15, ISO 14698 (cleanrooms).

How much does it cost?

Plate count ≈ US$20–60; microbial ID ≈ $80–300; efficacy studies ≈ $800–5,000 (indicative).

  1. Request quotes — describe your samples, test and deadline.
  2. Receive a sampling kit — containers, preservatives and chain-of-custody form.

Frequently asked questions

Why do I need environmental monitoring?

It verifies that cleaning and sanitation keep surfaces and air within limits, preventing product contamination — a core requirement of food safety and GMP programmes.

Can a lab substantiate an 'antibacterial' claim?

Yes, through standardised efficacy tests (e.g. ISO 22196, EN 1276). Claims are regulated, so check requirements in your target markets.
