Standards & accreditation

ASTM E8 (tensile), ASTM B117, ISO 6892, IEC 62321 (RoHS), EN/ISO product standards, CPSIA for children's products.

How much does it cost?

Tensile test ≈ US$50–250 per specimen; failure analysis ≈ $1,500–10,000; RoHS screen ≈ $100–400 (indicative).

  1. Request quotes — describe your samples, test and deadline.

Frequently asked questions

What is failure analysis?

A structured investigation — visual inspection, fractography, metallography, SEM/EDS and chemical analysis — to determine why a part broke, corroded or wore out, and how to prevent recurrence.

Can a lab test to my customer's specification?

Yes. Provide the standard (ASTM, ISO, EN or customer spec). Accredited labs list the methods they are accredited for on their scope of accreditation.

What is RoHS testing?

RoHS limits hazardous substances such as lead, mercury, cadmium, hexavalent chromium and certain flame retardants/phthalates in electrical and electronic equipment. XRF screening is typically followed by confirmatory wet chemistry.
