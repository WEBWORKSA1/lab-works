Frequently asked questions

Why is my potency result different from another lab's?

Sampling, homogenisation, moisture correction and method differences all affect results. Use ISO 17025-accredited labs and consistent sampling procedures.

Is hemp testing mandatory?

In most jurisdictions hemp must be tested pre-harvest for total THC; finished CBD products are subject to local labelling and safety rules.
